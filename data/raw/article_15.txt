Headline: Health Officials Warn of Waterborne Disease After Riverton
Floods Publication date: 2026-08-16 Source: The Riverton Herald

Health officials in Riverton warned residents of waterborne diseases
after the flooding. No outbreak had been reported as of Sunday, but
doctors expect cases of diarrhoea and fever to rise.

Medical teams were stationed at Central High School and the Riverside
Community Hall, as ordered at the Friday review meeting. Chlorine
tablets were distributed in flooded neighbourhoods.

Residents were advised to boil drinking water and avoid walking through
standing water. Officials began distributing drinking water and food
packets at both sites.

Mosquito control teams will begin fogging in Riverside on Monday. Health
officials asked people with fever to visit the nearest clinic.
