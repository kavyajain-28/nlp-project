Headline: Power Supply Restored in Parts of Flood-Hit Riverton
Publication date: 2026-08-15 Source: City News Network

Rainfall eased in Riverton on Saturday, August 15, but several
neighbourhoods continued to deal with flooding caused by the previous
day’s downpour. The weather office reported that the city had received
118 millimetres of rain in the twelve hours leading up to Friday
morning.

The municipal corporation said that 60 residents had used the temporary
shelters at Central High School and Riverside Community Hall. Officials
began distributing drinking water and food packets at both sites.

Power supply, which had been suspended in parts of Riverside as a safety
measure, was restored to most streets by 11 a.m. Repair crews continued
to inspect damaged electrical equipment.

The Old Bridge remained closed while engineers checked its structural
safety. The district collector said that a decision on reopening the
bridge would be made after the inspection. No deaths had been reported
as of Saturday afternoon.
