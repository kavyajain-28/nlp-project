Headline: Collector Calls Review Meeting as Schools Shut in Riverton
Publication date: 2026-08-14 Source: The Riverton Herald

Authorities closed all municipal schools in Riverton on Friday, August
14, because of the flooding. The decision was announced at 7:15 a.m.
after water levels in the Riverton River rose sharply overnight.

The district collector said that a review meeting would be held at 4
p.m. to assess the situation. Senior officials from the municipal
corporation, the police and the weather office were asked to attend.

The municipal corporation confirmed that temporary shelters had been set
up at Central High School and Riverside Community Hall. Teams were also
checking pumping stations in the eastern part of the city.

Private schools were asked to follow the closure order. The education
department said exams scheduled for Friday would be rescheduled.
