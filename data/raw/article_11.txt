Headline: Riverton Schools Likely to Reopen on Monday, Officials Say
Publication date: 2026-08-15 Source: Riverton Evening Post

Municipal schools in Riverton, which were closed on Friday, are likely
to reopen on Monday, the education department said. The final decision
depends on the weather and the condition of school buildings.

Central High School is being used as a temporary shelter and may need to
be vacated before classes resume. Officials said alternative
arrangements were being discussed.

Heavy rainfall continued in Riverton on Friday, August 14, leading
authorities to close all municipal schools for the day. Rainfall eased
on Saturday, but several neighbourhoods continued to deal with flooding.

Parents were asked to wait for an official announcement on Sunday
evening. Teachers have been asked to inspect classrooms on Sunday.
