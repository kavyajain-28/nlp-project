Headline: Riverton Schools Reopen After Flood Closure Publication date:
2026-08-18 Source: National Daily News

Municipal schools in Riverton reopened on Tuesday, August 18, after
remaining closed since the floods. Classes at Central High School
resumed after the building was cleaned and disinfected.

Attendance was low on the first day, with many students still dealing
with damage at home. Teachers said they would not hold tests this week.

Heavy rainfall continued in Riverton on Friday, August 14, leading
authorities to close all municipal schools for the day. The closure was
extended over the weekend and into Monday as shelters remained in use.

The education department said that missed lessons would be covered
through extra classes. Students who lost books were offered free
replacements.
