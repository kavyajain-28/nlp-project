Headline: Eastport Market Blaze Brought Under Control Publication date:
2026-08-17 Source: National Daily News

Firefighters brought a blaze at the Eastport fish market under control
by 5 a.m. on Monday. Six fire engines worked for nearly three hours to
put out the flames.

Fourteen stalls were destroyed before the flames were brought under
control. A fire officer said a short circuit was suspected.

The fire station chief said that stored ice and wooden crates fed the
flames. Water was drawn from a nearby canal after pressure dropped in
the main supply.

The market has been sealed while investigators inspect the site. Traders
were told to stay away until the area is declared safe.
