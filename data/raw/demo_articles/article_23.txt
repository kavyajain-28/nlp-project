Headline: Eastport Fire Probe Points to Faulty Wiring Publication date:
2026-08-19 Source: Eastport Gazette

An inquiry into the Eastport fish market fire found that faulty wiring
caused the blaze. The electricity board said old cables had overheated
in the stalls.

Fourteen stalls were destroyed before the flames were brought under
control. The council said the market would reopen in phases from
Thursday.

Traders will get temporary stalls on Harbour Road until the damaged
section is rebuilt. The council also promised to replace wiring across
the entire market.

A fire officer said the market had not had a safety inspection in two
years. The council said that inspections would now be held every six
months.
