Headline: Old Bridge Closed to Vehicles as Riverton River Swells
Publication date: 2026-08-14 Source: Metro Traffic Bulletin

The Old Bridge was closed to vehicles at around 6 a.m. as a precaution,
traffic police confirmed. Water in the Riverton River had risen sharply
overnight and was close to the bridge deck.

Police redirected traffic toward the North Bypass, which saw heavy
congestion through the morning. Buses on three city routes were rerouted
or cancelled.

Commuters were advised to avoid underpasses and roads near the river.
Several low-lying junctions in Riverside remained under water by noon.

Traffic officials said the bridge would stay closed until engineers
declared it safe. An update is expected after the review meeting on
Friday afternoon.
