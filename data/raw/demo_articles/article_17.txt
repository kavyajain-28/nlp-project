Headline: Relief Work Continues at Riverton Shelters Publication date:
2026-08-17 Source: Riverton Evening Post

Relief work continued on Monday at the shelters set up after the
Riverton floods. Officials said 28 residents were still staying at
Central High School.

The municipal corporation moved these residents to the Riverside
Community Hall on Monday evening so that the school can be cleaned.
Volunteers helped carry belongings and bedding.

Drinking water, food packets and medicines were available at the hall
round the clock. Doctors visited twice a day to check on elderly
residents.

The district collector visited the hall and spoke to affected families.
He said that compensation forms would be issued within the week.
