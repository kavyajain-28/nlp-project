Headline: Review Meeting Orders Food and Water for Riverton Flood
Victims Publication date: 2026-08-15 Source: National Daily News

The district collector held a review meeting on Friday at 4 p.m. to
assess the flood situation in Riverton. Officials from the municipal
corporation, police and weather office attended.

The meeting directed the municipal corporation to distribute drinking
water and food packets at all shelters. It also ordered medical teams to
be stationed at Central High School and Riverside Community Hall.

Officials said approximately 60 people had stayed at the two shelters on
Friday. The collector asked ward officers to survey damaged homes once
the water recedes.

Engineers were told to inspect the Old Bridge before it is reopened. The
weather office forecast more rain until Saturday evening.
