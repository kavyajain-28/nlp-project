Headline: Temporary Shelters Open as Floodwater Enters Riverside Homes
Publication date: 2026-08-14 Source: Riverton Evening Post

Floodwater entered ground-floor homes in the Riverside neighbourhood on
Friday morning after continuous rainfall overnight. Emergency teams
helped families leave low-lying streets before dawn.

The municipal corporation opened two temporary shelters at Central High
School and the Riverside Community Hall. Officials said that 46
residents had been moved to the shelters by 10 a.m.

Volunteers from nearby wards brought blankets, mats and cooked meals to
both locations. By noon, approximately 60 people were staying at the two
locations.

Police advised residents to stay away from the river bank and flooded
underpasses. The weather office said more rain was likely through
Saturday.
