Headline: Power Restored to All Riverside Streets After Flood Repairs
Publication date: 2026-08-16 Source: City News Network

Power supply was restored to the remaining streets of Riverside on
Sunday, the electricity board said. Repair crews had finished inspecting
damaged electrical equipment late on Saturday.

Power supply, which had been suspended in parts of Riverside as a safety
measure, was restored to most streets by 11 a.m. on Saturday. The
remaining streets needed new cables after water damaged a transformer.

Engineers replaced one transformer and four street-level junction boxes.
The electricity board said it had deployed 12 repair crews across the
eastern part of the city.

Residents were asked not to touch wires or switches in flooded homes
until they are checked. A helpline was opened for complaints.
