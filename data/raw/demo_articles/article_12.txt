Headline: Old Bridge Reopens to Light Vehicles After Inspection
Publication date: 2026-08-16 Source: Metro Traffic Bulletin

The Old Bridge in Riverton was reopened to light vehicles on Sunday
morning after engineers completed their inspection. Heavy vehicles and
buses remain barred until further tests are done.

The district collector said that the structure had not suffered serious
damage. Minor cracks in the approach road were repaired overnight.

Police removed the diversion toward the North Bypass for cars and
two-wheelers. Traffic officers will monitor water levels in the Riverton
River throughout the day.

Bus services on three routes resumed on Sunday afternoon using the North
Bypass. Officials said normal bus movement over the bridge would be
allowed after a load test.
