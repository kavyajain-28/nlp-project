Headline: Riverside Residents Return to Damaged Homes as Water Recedes
Publication date: 2026-08-15 Source: City News Network

Water began to recede from the Riverside neighbourhood on Saturday
morning as rainfall eased. Many families returned to find furniture and
household goods damaged by the flood.

The municipal corporation said that 60 residents had used the temporary
shelters at Central High School and Riverside Community Hall. Most of
them had returned home by Saturday evening, while those with unsafe
homes stayed on.

Power supply, which had been suspended in parts of Riverside as a safety
measure, was restored to most streets by 11 a.m. Residents said the
supply was steady through the afternoon.

Ward officers began recording damage to homes and shops. Families were
asked to keep photographs of the damage for compensation claims.
