Headline: Fire Breaks Out at Eastport Fish Market Publication date:
2026-08-17 Source: Eastport Gazette

A fire broke out at the Eastport fish market at around 2:10 a.m. on
Monday, August 17. Fourteen stalls were destroyed before the flames were
brought under control.

Six fire engines were sent to the market from Eastport and the
neighbouring town of Harbourview. Firefighters struggled at first
because narrow lanes blocked access.

No injuries were reported, as the market was closed at the time. Police
cordoned off the area and diverted traffic on Harbour Road.

Traders arrived at dawn to find their stock burned. Officials said the
cause of the fire was not yet known.
