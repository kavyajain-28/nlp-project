Headline: Traders Demand Relief After Eastport Market Fire Publication
date: 2026-08-18 Source: City News Network

Traders at the Eastport fish market demanded compensation on Tuesday
after a fire destroyed 14 stalls. They said they lost stock and
equipment worth several lakh rupees.

A fire officer said that a short circuit was suspected. The electricity
board has been asked to submit a report on the wiring at the market.

The Eastport municipal council said it would provide temporary space for
traders on Harbour Road. Police said there were no injuries in the fire,
which began at around 2:10 a.m. on Monday.

Traders' association leaders met the council chairman on Tuesday
afternoon. They asked for interest-free loans to restart their
businesses.
