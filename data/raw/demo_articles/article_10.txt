Headline: Engineers Inspect Old Bridge as Riverton Waits to Reopen It
Publication date: 2026-08-15 Source: The Riverton Herald

The Old Bridge remained closed while engineers checked its structural
safety. A team from the public works department examined the pillars and
approach roads on Saturday.

The district collector said that a decision on reopening the bridge
would be made after the inspection. Officials expected a preliminary
report by Sunday morning.

No deaths had been reported as of Saturday afternoon. Two people were
treated for minor injuries after slipping on flooded roads.

Repair crews continued to inspect damaged electrical equipment. Traffic
continued to use the North Bypass, where police deployed extra
personnel.
