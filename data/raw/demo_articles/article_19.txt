Headline: State Announces Compensation for Flood-Hit Families in
Riverton Publication date: 2026-08-18 Source: The Riverton Herald

The state government announced compensation for families whose homes
were damaged in the Riverton floods. Each eligible household will
receive 10,000 rupees as immediate relief.

An initial survey by the municipal corporation found that 312 homes were
damaged in the Riverton floods. Officials said the list could grow as
more claims arrive.

The district collector said that forms would be issued at ward offices
from Wednesday. Families must submit photographs of the damage and proof
of address.

Opposition members said the amount was too small to cover repairs. The
government said that larger claims would be assessed separately.
