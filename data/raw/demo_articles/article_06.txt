Headline: Weather Office Warns Riverton of More Rain Through Saturday
Publication date: 2026-08-14 Source: Regional Weather Review

The city weather office recorded 118 millimetres of rain in twelve
hours, the highest twelve-hour total in Riverton this monsoon.
Continuous rainfall began late on August 13 and continued into Friday
morning.

The weather office warned that further rainfall was expected through
Saturday. Forecasters said a slow-moving system over the coast was
feeding the downpour.

Residents were advised to avoid underpasses and roads near the river.
Farmers on the outskirts were asked to delay harvesting until the rain
eased.

The Riverton River crossed its warning mark overnight, according to the
irrigation department. Officials said levels would be checked every two
hours.
