Headline: Riverton Counts the Cost of Flood Damage to Homes and Shops
Publication date: 2026-08-17 Source: Regional Business Review

An initial survey by the municipal corporation found that 312 homes were
damaged in the Riverton floods. Dozens of shops in the eastern part of
the city also reported stock losses.

The corporation put the first estimate of damage at 40 crore rupees.
Officials said the figure could change as the survey continues.

Continuous rainfall began across Riverton late on August 13, causing
several streets in the eastern part of the city to flood by early Friday
morning. The city weather office recorded 118 millimetres of rain in
twelve hours.

Traders said they were waiting for insurance assessors to visit. The
chamber of commerce asked the state government for a relief package.
