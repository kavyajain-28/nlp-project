Headline: Schools Reopening Delayed as Riverton Shelter Stays Open
Publication date: 2026-08-16 Source: National Daily News

The education department said that municipal schools in Riverton will
now reopen on Tuesday instead of Monday. Central High School is still
being used as a temporary shelter.

Officials said that 28 residents whose homes remain unsafe are still
staying at the shelter. The remaining families have moved back to
Riverside or to relatives' homes.

The municipal corporation said it would shift those residents to the
Riverside Community Hall by Monday evening. Classrooms at Central High
School will then be cleaned and disinfected.

Parents expressed concern about missed lessons. The department said
extra classes would be arranged later in the month.
