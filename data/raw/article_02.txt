Headline: Schools Closed as Riverton River Level Rises Publication date:
2026-08-14 Source: National Daily News

Heavy rainfall continued in Riverton on Friday, August 14, leading
authorities to close all municipal schools for the day. The decision was
announced at 7:15 a.m. after water levels in the Riverton River rose
sharply overnight.

The Old Bridge was closed to vehicles at around 6 a.m. as a precaution.
Police redirected traffic toward the North Bypass, while emergency teams
helped residents leave low-lying homes in Riverside.

The municipal corporation confirmed that temporary shelters had been set
up at Central High School and Riverside Community Hall. By noon,
approximately 60 people were staying at the two locations.

The district collector said that a review meeting would be held at 4
p.m. to assess the situation. The weather office forecast more rain
until Saturday evening.
